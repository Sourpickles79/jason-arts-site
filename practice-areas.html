Featured transformation · Oakview House

Dark rooms out.
Everyday light in.

A fragmented 1980s plan became a warm, connected kitchen and living space without losing the home’s original rhythm.

ScopeKitchen, living room, custom millwork
Timeline18 fictional weeks
MaterialsWhite oak, limestone, blackened steel

The design move

One structural opening changed the entire house.

Instead of removing every wall, the design widened a single connection and used a continuous material palette to pull the rooms together.

Storage moved into a full-height oak wall. The oversized stone island became the social center, while new windows carried afternoon light deep into the plan.
